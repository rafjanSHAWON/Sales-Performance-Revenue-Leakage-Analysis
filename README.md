📊 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics (PostgreSQL)

🧠 Project Overview:

This project focuses on transforming raw, messy e-commerce transactional data into actionable business insights using PostgreSQL.
The analysis answers key business questions related to revenue growth, discount strategy, customer behavior, product performance, and regional sales trends.

The goal is to demonstrate end-to-end analytical thinking — from data preparation to KPI design and insight generation — exactly how a Business Intelligence / Data Analyst works in real-world scenarios.

🎯 Business Problem:

The business had access to order, customer, and product data but lacked clarity on:

How revenue is distributed across time, products, and regions

Whether discounts are increasing or hurting overall revenue

Which customers and products drive the most value

How customer purchasing behavior impacts long-term growth

Decision-makers needed reliable KPIs and insights to improve pricing, marketing, and sales strategy.

🧩 Dataset Description:

The analysis uses multiple related tables, including:

orders

order_items

customers

products

regions

Each table contains real-world issues such as:

NULL values

Duplicate records

Inconsistent text formatting

Discounted pricing logic

Time-based data (order dates)

🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Before analysis, the following steps were performed:

Validated table relationships and data integrity

Removed duplicate records

Handled NULL and missing values

Standardized text fields (status, categories, regions)

Calculated accurate revenue using quantity, price, and discount

Created time-based dimensions (year, month)

This ensured that all KPIs were built on trustworthy and consistent data.

🔍 Key Insights
1️⃣ Discount Strategy Impact

Low to medium discounts generated higher total revenue

Heavy discounts increased order volume but reduced net revenue

📌 Insight: Optimized discounting is more effective than aggressive price cuts.

2️⃣ Revenue Concentration

A small group of customers contributed a large percentage of total revenue

High-value customers showed repeat purchase behavior

📌 Insight: Strong opportunity for loyalty programs and retention strategies.

3️⃣ Product & Category Performance

Certain products and categories consistently outperformed others

A few products acted as revenue anchors

📌 Insight: Inventory and marketing should prioritize top-performing products.

4️⃣ Regional Trends

Sales were uneven across regions and countries

Some regions showed higher order density and revenue

📌 Insight: Regional marketing and logistics optimization can improve ROI.

5️⃣ Time-Based Trends

Monthly and yearly revenue patterns revealed seasonal behavior

📌 Insight: Useful for forecasting and campaign planning.
